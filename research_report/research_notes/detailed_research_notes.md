**Title**: Recent Advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Their Societal Impacts

### Introduction:    

The field of artificial intelligence has witnessed exponential growth over recent years with AI becoming an integral part in various sectors such as healthcare, transportation, finance and even entertainment. This research paper aims to delve into the recent advancements made in Artificial Intelligence (AI) while examining their impact on society at large by referring primarily from reputable sources like peer-reviewed articles published within the last 3 years.

### Section I: Current Technologies and Techniques  
#### Subsection A - Natural Language Processing Advancements   
* Recent developments in natural language processing (NLP) include transformer neural networks such as BERT, developed by Google that can understand context better than any prior model. This is backed up with a recent study on PubMed which showed their effectiveness and efficiency over older models like LSTMs [1].
* Transfer learning has been an instrumental technique for enhancing AI capabilities in tasks such as language translation, sentiment analysis etc., by reusing pre-trained models. An article from ScienceDirect highlighted this method's efficacy recently[2].  
  ### Subsection B - Improved Machine Learning Techniques   
* Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) and Recurrent Neural Network(RNN), as per a study published on arXiv, have shown significant improvements in image recognition tasks. CNNs are still the best choice for visual data processing [3].  
  ### Subsection C - Evolution of Reinforcement Learning   
* The introduction of Actor-Critic methods has provided promising results by combining actor and critic networks to maximize rewards more efficiently in reinforcement learning[4] as per a recent paper published on Nature.    

### Section II: Recent Developments in Specific Fields  
#### Subsection D - Healthcare   
* Advances like AI-assisted diagnosis have been promising, with studies from IEEE showcasing improved accuracy rates for certain types of diseases[5]. 
* In radiology and oncology, deep learning techniques are being applied to create algorithms that accurately detect abnormalities in medical images [6], as per a study published in JAMA.  
#### Subsection E - Transportation   
* Self-driving cars have become more advanced with AI's ability to predict and navigate the road, improving safety significantly[7] according to an article from Nature Machine Intelligence. 
* Lastly, we find smart traffic management systems using reinforcement learning algorithms which can optimize city congestion as per a study published in PLOS ONE [8].  
#### Subsection F - Entertainment    
* Reinforcement Learning has been used to create adaptive and personalized video games[9], providing an engaging gaming experience. 
* Also, virtual reality technology combined with AI allows more immersive experiences as per a study from Nature Communications [10].  
#### Subsection G - Finance    
* Algorithmic Trading has become much faster and accurate using reinforcement learning[11] according to research published in IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks. 
* AI is also used for fraud detection, as per a study from SpringerLink [12].  
#### Subsection H - Social Media Interaction    
* Chatbots powered by transformer-based neural networks have revolutionized customer service and interactions[13] according to an article in the Journal of Marketing Research.   
### Section III: Ethical Implications, Limitations & Future Trends  
#### Subsection I - Societal Concerns    
* There are ethical concerns like data privacy issues as AI becomes more powerful [14]. Also discussed is potential job displacement[15], with solutions suggested in a study published on arXiv. 
### Section IV: Conclusion  
Recent advancements in artificial intelligence have led to significant developments across various industries, providing benefits and raising ethical concerns simultaneously. As AI continues evolving at an exponential pace it is important that we address its societal impacts with equal urgency while leveraging these technologies for the betterment of society as a whole[16].
