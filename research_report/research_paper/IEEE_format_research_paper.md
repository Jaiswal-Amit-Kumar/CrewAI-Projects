
**Title**: Recent Advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Their Societal Impacts  

### Abstract ###
This research paper provides an extensive review of the recent advancements made in artificial intelligence, primarily focusing on Natural Language Processing(NLP), Improved Machine Learning Techniques as well as their specific developments across sectors such as healthcare, transportation and entertainment. The report further delves into ethical implications arising from these AI advancements along with limitations present in the technology thus far while also examining potential future trends based on current knowledge up to 2023 [1].

### Introduction ###  
The field of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has seen a rapid growth, becoming instrumental across various sectors. This paper will explore these developments and their societal impacts by referring primarily from reputable sources such as peer-reviewed articles published within the last 3 years[2]. It is essential to recognize that while AI advancements have brought about substantial benefits in diverse areas, they also raise ethical considerations which need prompt attention [3].

### Section I: Current Technologies and Techniques ###  
#### Subsection A - Natural Language Processing Advancements #### 
* Transformer neural networks such as BERT have emerged with the capability to understand context better than any prior models[4]. Studies show its effectiveness over traditional LSTMs in various tasks, making it a breakthrough development [5].  
#### Subsection B - Improved Machine Learning Techniques #### 
* Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) and Recurrent Neural Network(RNN), according to studies on arXiv[6], have shown significant improvements in image recognition tasks, with CNNs still being the best choice for visual data processing [7].  
#### Subsection C - Evolution of Reinforcement Learning #### 
* The introduction of Actor-Critic methods has provided promising results by combining actor and critic networks to maximize rewards efficiently in reinforcement learning[8] as per a recent paper published on Nature.   
### Section II: Recent Developments in Specific Fields ###  
#### Subsection D - Healthcare #### 
* Advances like AI-assisted diagnosis have shown promising results, with studies indicating improved accuracy rates for certain types of diseases[9]. Deep learning techniques are being applied to create algorithms that accurately detect abnormalities in medical images [10] as per a study published on JAMA.  
#### Subsection E - Transportation #### 
* Self-driving cars have become more advanced, with AI's ability to predict and navigate roads significantly improving safety[11]. Furthermore, smart traffic management systems using reinforcement learning algorithms optimize city congestion as shown in a study published on PLOS ONE [12] .  
#### Subsection F - Entertainment #### 
* Reinforcement Learning has been used to create adaptive and personalized video games[13], providing an engaging gaming experience. Virtual reality technology combined with AI allows for more immersive experiences as per a study from Nature Communications [14].  
#### Subsection G - Finance #### 
* Algorithmic Trading has become faster, accurate using reinforcement learning according to research published in IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks[15] . AI is also used for fraud detection as per a study from SpringerLink [16].  
#### Subsection H - Social Media Interaction #### 
* Chatbots powered by transformer-based neural networks have revolutionized customer service and interactions, according to an article in the Journal of Marketing Research[17] .   
### Section III: Ethical Implications, Limitations & Future Trends ###  
#### Subsection I - Societal Concerns #### 
* As AI becomes more powerful, ethical concerns like data privacy issues arise [18]. The technology may potentially displace jobs[19] but this can be mitigated by providing alternative employment options and skill development as suggested in a study published on arXiv.   
### Conclusion ###  
Recent advancements in artificial intelligence have led to significant developments across various industries, offering benefits while also raising ethical concerns simultaneously [20]. As AI continues evolving at an exponential pace it is critical that we address its societal impacts with equal urgency and leverage these technologies for the betterment of society as a whole[21].
